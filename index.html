The keep

Everything a manager needs to hold the line.

Not another minimalist inbox. Castellum is a command HQ — email, project work, and AI triage in one fortified place.

Active Work, not just unread

A built-in kanban turns threads into tracked follow-ups. Surface overdue replies, prep for meetings, and never let a project email slip through the portcullis.

Local AI engine

Summaries, triage and draft replies via Ollama on your own machine. No email content leaves your Mac.

Gmail in one click

Sign in with Google instantly — or connect Outlook, iCloud, Fastmail and any server through manual IMAP/SMTP.

Keyboard-first

Fly through your inbox with j/k navigation, / to search, and a command palette for everything else.

Projects, contacts & calendar

Link emails to project spaces, notes and people — with an agenda view and meeting-prep summaries beside your mail.

Compliance-first by default

Local-only AI mode is on from the first launch. Sensitive content stays on-device, you choose if and when anything cloud-based is ever allowed. Built for people who can't send the inbox to a third party.

Why a castle

Your inbox is yours. Full stop.

Most "AI email" tools ship your mail to someone else's servers to work. Castellum is built the opposite way: the walls keep your data in, and the AI comes to it.

  • AI runs on-device with Ollama — no email sent to the cloud.
  • Mail and notes stored in a local SQLite database on your Mac.
  • Signed with an Apple Developer ID and notarized — Gatekeeper-approved.
  • No tracking, no account, no telemetry you didn't ask for.
