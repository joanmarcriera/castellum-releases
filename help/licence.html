Licence and trial

Settings > Licence · Castellum

Open Settings > Licence to see your status and manage your key.

1. The 14-day trial

Castellum starts with a 14-day trial. The Licence tab shows “Trial: N days remaining”.

2. Activate a key

  1. Click Activate….
  2. Enter the licence key from your purchase confirmation. It looks like CASTM-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX.
  3. Click Activate. If something is wrong the sheet stays open so you can fix a typo and try again.

3. Status messages

ShownMeaning
Trial: N days remainingYou are in the 14-day trial
Trial expired — enter a licence key to continueThe trial has ended
LicensedYour key is active (with a “valid until” date if it has one)
Licence check failed — running on a 14-day graceThe licence server could not be reached; see section 4
Licence expired — enter a licence key to continueYour licence has ended

Deactivate (shown when you have a licence) removes the key from this Mac. Check Licence re-checks your licence now; Castellum does not re-check on a timer.

4. If the licence server cannot be reached

Castellum keeps working for 14 days from the last successful check, and the tab shows how many days are left.

In this build, nothing is switched off. Castellum works fully during the trial and while a licence is active, and no features are disabled when a trial or licence lapses. This may change in a future version, and this page will be updated when it does.
